- Pure static site (no build, no deps). Three.js r128 and Google Fonts load from CDN.
- Served by python http.server in `docker-compose.base44.yml` with the repo bind-mounted read-only; edits are live on browser reload (no HMR).
- Verify: `curl localhost:3000/` returns index.html; game needs WebGL in the browser.
- Cars are discovered from the Python server's `assets/cars/` directory listing; hosts without listings need explicit entries in `js/assets.js`. Non-police/non-van vehicles randomly share the loaded sports pool. The supplied Alfa Romeo is registered explicitly with a small X tilt correction; its GLB wheel geometry was split into four named assemblies and separate badge meshes were named for removal. Niko is the player; Claude and Alien share the pedestrian pool. Niko (ValveBiped) and Alien (Mixamo) have only zero-duration pose clips, filtered out by Assets.clone. Their manifest proceduralMotion flag enables js/character-motion.js: cloned bone rest poses, lowered arms, local-axis walking/running/knee/punch motion. Claude remains static; Lucia is retained on disk but unused. `assets/trevor.glb` remains unused. Index 0 is the player, other people entries are NPCs. Car forward axes must be authored +Z or declared in the manifest; bounds cannot determine the nose.
- Vehicles.sampleFrame measures uncapped frame time even in the menu; a one-second FPS average below 45 enables procedural sports-car proxies for distant moving traffic only. Player and parked cars remain detailed; 80/100m and 45/50 FPS margins prevent LOD flicker.
- Load Assets before World.build, not only before vehicles: building placement needs the cache. Model selection uses a separate RNG so skipped procedural visuals still consume the original world RNG sequence.
- Three.js r128 instanced building batches disable frustum culling because that version does not compute instance-aware bounds; animation uses SkeletonUtils clones and per-character mixers.
- London is a single complete neighborhood, not a building-lot asset. `js/neighborhood.js` crops outlying terrain, hides invisible/lightmap helpers, uses connected-component building bounds and samples imported street heights. A bridge at x=80 connects the north perimeter; original grid/traffic/missions are unchanged. The export has multiple road terraces: y=-10 is the lower deck, with other large surfaces at y=0. js/london-foundation.js fills exposed raised-road boundary gaps down to the base slab, preserving bridge/tunnel openings; connected building components receive foundation walls down to the slab. Do not lower the entire district to fix upper terraces: that buries the lower streets. Both maps include London; bridge routing is supported, internal London GPS is only a direct line. Verify map waypoint clicks and an unobstructed north seawall opening at (80,-488.6). Traversal through the full district still needs a manual gameplay check.
- Supplied Niko hand POSITION vertices were exported at 100x the scale of the rest of his body; assets/people/niko.glb corrects that buffer and its accessor bounds by dividing by 100. Do not re-download over this fix without applying the correction.
- r128 Box3 ignores skinned poses. Assets.modelBounds measures bone-transformed vertices before character normalization: Lucia's scaled armature otherwise makes her render about five times too small; Claude's skinned waist also needs posed bounds.
